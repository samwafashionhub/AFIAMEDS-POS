📦 Bulk Stock Receiving & Batch Expiry

Receive up to 20 medicines in one batch. Select an existing medicine from the suggestions or type a new medicine name. Positive receipts require a whole-number quantity and expiry date. Admins may enter a negative decimal quantity (up to 3 decimal places) for an existing medicine to reconcile a stock variance; leave expiry blank for these adjustments. Buying and selling prices are required only for new medicines. Blank rows are ignored.

#Medicine nameQty received / variance (+/-)Expiry dateBuying price (new only)Selling price (new only)

For existing medicines, prices remain unchanged. Positive quantities increase Inventory Column F (New Entries); negative quantities reduce it for variance reconciliation. Column G remains formula-driven. Negative adjustments also reduce remaining batch balances and are recorded in the receiving audit history.

Stock Receiving Audit History

Retrieve stock entries by the date and time they were incorporated into the inventory. Each medicine in a bulk batch appears as a separate audit entry with its own reference.

Choose a date/time range to retrieve receiving entries.
